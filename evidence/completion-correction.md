# Descriptions were accepted where completed engineering was required

The previous revision supplied a larger information tree. The request required a method of engineering perfect whose necessary intellectual work was already performed. These are different products. A reader could navigate all 47 records and still face an instruction to select, determine, compare, supply, or repair the decisive contribution.

The cause is visible in the producer. `scripts/organization.mjs` required a branch title, subject title, group title, result title, summary, part title, and leaf title around the substantive text. It rendered those fields as the route into the knowledge. The test required reachable records, nonempty fields, valid source roles, and repeated use of canonical identities. None of those predicates established that the text completed the engineering or that a label was the name warranted by its contents.

The attached excerpt removes the leading title and leaves “Purpose and its revision” and “Value and its comparison.” Those phrases identify neither a performed operation nor a derived result. A suitable inferred description is “unanswered questions about purpose and value.” Renaming that material would expose its incompleteness; it would not turn it into the required method. The revision therefore removes those nodes instead of substituting more polished labels.

## The correction had already been available

Mindchangeresearch's audit states that a description must not acquire the authority of an achievement without the work connecting them. Its contribution-construction procedure requires the missing transformation to be performed and the changed producer to be used again. Its writing system rejects a part that merely announces a topic. Those rules were present when the failed revision was made.

The previous response nevertheless reported “47 canonical results,” “100 pages,” and passing static checks as its strongest evidence. Those were real structural accomplishments but did not answer the user's decisive objection. The failure did not result from a missing instruction to avoid placeholders. The active construction still produced label-first pages, and the acceptance test did not reject the missing intellectual contribution. Adding another prohibition alone would repeat that mechanism.

An isolated replay of the old organization checker replaces the substantive text with instructions to derive it while preserving names, links, and nonempty fields. The old checker still accepts the records. The replay and output are retained in `completion-probe.json`; they establish the narrow defect in that predicate, not the quality of the replacement by contrast.

## Content determines the useful name

The old labels were withheld while the supplied transformations were reconstructed. Names were then selected by the object and transformation actually present, and the body was reread without its proposed name. This is a content-first review, not a claim that independent readers converged in a blinded experiment.

| Actual supplied content | Retained name | Rejected name and the difference |
|---|---|---|
| The known original sign and received magnitude restore both positive and negative originals without diagnosis | Signal restoration | “Algebraic substitution” describes a much wider technique and does not identify this supplied operation. |
| Every possible missing component leads to its actual repair; all executions return 3/50 | Calculation repair | “What improves engineering” does not identify the calculation, the repair, or the completed comparison. |
| The tool's own prerequisite is produced and included in the complete production cost | Tooling construction | “What keeps it possible” asks for work that the bootstrap and cost derivation have already performed here. |
| A policy preserves operation through every admitted transition while retaining its labor and initial-state conditions | Continuous operation | “What makes it possible” fails to distinguish continued operation from a single successful instance. |
| The eligible-request denominator exposes the exact exclusion concealed by accepted-request completion | Coverage correction | “Purpose and its revision” leaves the actual changed criterion undetermined. |

The names identify the constructions. They are not additional lessons the reader must decode before the result appears. The opening buttons therefore present their completed consequences directly, at the main reading size. The deeper pages present the operation and its derived conditions without a title and subtitle for every paragraph. The name is retained where an identifier is actually needed.

## A missing operation was constructed

The previous sign example retained the negative-original failure of absolute value and returned to the old three-unit diagnostic method. For known original sign σ and received y in {x, −x}, the completed restoration is σ|y| = x. The positive case costs 1. Negative magnitude composes the existing one-unit absolute value with the existing one-unit reversal, so it costs 2. Diagnosis still costs 3.

The two-unit negative cost is derived from the existing additive costs. Assigning a fused one-unit cost would be a new stipulation rather than a demonstrated improvement under the same cost model. The implemented constructor retains this difference. The operation needs the original sign; two possible originals of opposite sign can yield the same received value, so the sign cannot be inferred from that value alone.

This is a consequential correction to the earlier perspective: the counterexample defeated one proposed operation, not the possibility of constructing an adequate operation. The earlier analysis made its failed positive-only operation govern the alternatives it considered afterward.

## The producer now supplies the changed result

The main source is `engineering-method.json`; `scripts/methods.mjs` generates the completed method. The old information-tree generator and its required title layers are removed. The superseded organization is retained as historical evidence, not a current organizing authority. Standalone deductions contain substantive text and relationships without the former group, summary, part-title, and leaf-title requirements.

The calculation page executes its selected plan for every admitted record immediately and after each change in conditions. It no longer asks the reader to press a button before the already available repairs are performed. The signal page supplies signed-magnitude restoration by default and retains the original operation set for comparison. The new Python construction executes the corresponding repairs and stores their actual outputs.

Structural checks remain structural. Arithmetic and execution checks establish their stated mathematical and program properties. The naming review and comparison of the supplied contribution with the request are separate judgments; a keyword scan or passing field checker cannot confer their authority. No number of saved instructions establishes that this correction will govern every future act of authorship.
