# What engineerperfect adds to the existing repositories

The missing contribution is a developed body of engineering constructions connecting worthwhile conditions to their actual production, continued operation, repair, and reproduction. Several predecessors already name or derive parts of this problem. Claiming they address only thoughts or beliefs would misread their explicit scope.

The comparison used complete untruncated file trees and selected source documents from six populated repositories. The [source record](evidence/source-review.json) pins commits and retrieved files. It supports a focused comparison of inspected work, not an exhaustive claim that no similar idea occurs elsewhere in thousands of files.

## The gaps are specific constructions

| Previously available distinction | Additional determination supplied here |
|---|---|
| A representation does not produce its referent | Exact conditions separating reachable states, maintainable states, and states from which both can be guaranteed |
| An executor may lack a needed capability | An invariant computation showing that no dispatch policy can compensate for absent maintenance concurrency |
| Information must distinguish situations requiring different actions | Preserving-action intersections derived from dynamics, with a cheaper sufficient sensor and a narrower requirement that needs more distinctions |
| Individually valid components can fail together | Necessary and sufficient clearance bounds, including process variation, use-condition drift, and measurement uncertainty |
| Capability investment can improve later outcomes | Full cost functions, maintenance timing, bootstrap feasibility, and exact demand thresholds |
| A broader perspective can reveal new possibilities | A developed comparison showing which changed system boundary supplies which new intervention |
| A criterion can reward a substitute for success | An explicit accepted-job denominator counterexample, with the missing service obligation preserved |
| Corrections must govern actual later production | An implemented rejection of empty successor sets, checked against the exact defect it prevents |

These are extensions and applications, not claims to have invented systems engineering or control theory. [NASA's product-realization account](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/5-0-product-realization/) already distinguishes construction and qualification activities. The contribution here is their connection to this research program through completed particular constructions.

## How the standard changed this repository's construction

The initial interpretation could have produced a general engineering framework with fields for a desired result, mechanism, and verification. That would leave mechanism construction in an empty intellectual position. The selected form instead places completed derivations and examples at direct paths, with code implementing the finite selection operations.

Design alternative construction was applied by retaining the six readings of the name and by varying sensors, mechanics, manufacturing, staffing, and tooling in the actual examples. Mind Change Research's local consequence-construction procedure was applied to derive the controller, feasibility interval, resource bound, and investment thresholds. Its capability-construction procedure supplied the executable selector and counterexamples. Its correction-retention procedure governed subsequent prose and code: results are not promoted by passed formatting checks, malformed transitions are not counted as success, and unobserved physical effects remain unobserved.

These are concrete uses of the relevant Subject Systems contributions. They are not claims to have executed original Reasoningtool skills or GOSM runs. The predecessor's old application counts and allocation obligations do not become new engineering achievements by copying them.

## Remaining determinations are not silently reassigned to the reader

The completed mathematical work assumes explicit dynamics, ranges, and resource costs. Physical realization still lacks measured pump behavior, demonstrated process capability at the proposed tolerances, and observed service and tooling costs. The equations identify what those quantities would change. No source supplied those measurements, and no external device was operated.

The finite solver does not yet construct policies using hidden-state histories, continuous mechanics, or learning from unknown dynamics. Those capabilities are not prerequisites to the demonstrated cases, and they are not advertised as implemented. The general ambition also requires treatment of changing purposes, competing legitimate demands, and forms of engineering not represented by these cases. This collection does not establish an exhaustive engineering domain or a universal perfection certificate.
