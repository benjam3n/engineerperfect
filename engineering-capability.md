# Engineering capability

An engineer's available capability belongs to the person or model together with the tools, material, access, representations, time, and collaborators actually usable in the undertaking. A capability claim that silently includes expert intervention, an unavailable instrument, or unlimited repair time describes a different arrangement.

Mind-change work must determine which of these contributions is missing. A person may need to notice an overlooked route, understand why a proposed route fails, acquire a practiced operation, or gain access to a resource. These changes can support one another but are not interchangeable. The service case below requires concurrent repair; the water case instead permits a better information choice. Neither justifies a universal instruction to think harder or add more resources.

Let C(A,E) be the set of undertakings actor A can accomplish in environment E under specified demands. Training A and changing E can each change C. The same observed improvement does not identify which one supplied it. An operation completed with a tool demonstrates supported performance; independent performance after removing the tool is a separate claim.

This definition does not assume capability has a universal scalar score. One arrangement can handle more disturbances while another works with less equipment. Unless the undertaking provides a way to resolve that trade-off, declaring one universally superior loses the distinction.

## More intelligence cannot compensate for a missing causal route

In the [service model](examples/service-during-repair.md), one worker must use every period to meet immediate service demand. No period remains for repair. Every service consumes finite remaining machine capacity. Since total initial capacity is four jobs and none is restored, the fifth job fails regardless of the worker's prediction, explanation, or dispatch rule.

The impossibility is specific: the actor is constrained to that labor arrangement and those transitions. Another repair mechanism, an available helper, a different deadline, or more durable machines can change it. It is not a statement that the task is impossible under all engineering.

The result identifies the smallest relevant category of change: restore capacity without displacing required service, reduce capacity consumption, or legitimately alter the demand. It rules out further dispatch optimization as a complete repair under the unchanged premises.

## Better judgment can prevent unnecessary construction

In the water model, a fine sensor is unnecessary for maintaining the wider acceptable band because two observation classes already have common preserving actions. The [constructed policy](examples/water-level-control.md) therefore removes the need to purchase information that the requirement does not use.

For the narrower band, the required distinctions change. Retaining the cheap sensor solely because it was previously adequate would now be the error. Engineering judgment preserves the relationship between the requirement and the required capability, rather than declaring either simplicity or sophistication universally preferable.

The same test applies to a response or representation. More details are useful when they preserve an action-changing distinction, justify an inference, or make a needed operation possible. Removing all explanation can leave the receiving actor unable to use the result. Repeated framing can consume attention without adding a needed distinction. The work must determine which contribution each piece makes.

## Improving the engineer includes improving correction

A consequential objection can require changing the engineer's proposal rather than the other person's acceptance of it. In the service case, an objection identifying the labor deficit defeats a dispatch-only repair. A procedure that increases agreement while retaining that repair leaves the engineering defect intact. The [examined change method](unoptimized-mind-change-methods.md) makes this distinction explicit through a counterexample.

Suppose a construction depends on premise p. Evidence defeats p, but the active generator continues using it. The record of the correction has changed while the engineering operation has not. A correct new paragraph leaves the recurrence intact.

A usable correction must reach the actual consumer of p. If the consumer is code, change the computation and examine a case whose result depends on that change. If it is a human skill, a written correction establishes availability of an explanation; later performance is needed to establish uptake. If it is a dependency outside available access, the current guarantee must disclose that dependency.

The present implementation applies this distinction to a concrete failure mode: an empty successor set is rejected rather than treated as universal success. A regression check calls the function with that exact malformed model. This confirms that the specific computational consumer rejects the defect. It does not certify the absence of every reasoning placeholder in the repository.

The organization correction has a different receiving operation. A separate institution-shaped program would direct future content selection away from the governing mind-change purpose. The revised README and contribution instructions now select and arrange the existing subject work by the useful changes it supplies; the two critique subjects identify specific corrections inside that work. This is a change in the current producer and its outputs. A future contributor's continued application remains a separate matter.

## A method can reduce dependence on its author

The finite control code takes explicit states, actions, and possible successors and returns maintainable states and usable policies. Its central selection operation is implemented. A future user need not invent the correct controller after reading a card that says to choose one.

However, the code does not discover physical dynamics, determine what people should value, or certify the accuracy of an input model. Claiming those capabilities would move the unresolved work into its inputs and conceal it there. The examples expose their model construction and interpretation, including the assumptions that limit transfer.

The resulting gain is precise: once a finite model with the stated conditions is supplied, the controller construction and its limits are available as an executable operation. The repository has not demonstrated a general improvement in the author's engineering skill, the user's skill, or any model's performance across later tasks.

## Engineering a worthwhile arrangement preserves agency where agency matters

A process can efficiently obtain a person's outward agreement while undermining their access to alternatives. If the undertaking includes informed and revisable participation, the outward result is insufficient. A design that depends on continued consent must retain a functioning way to change or withdraw it within the actual commitments involved.

This does not resolve every conflict among people's interests. It blocks one specific substitution: control over another person's conduct does not by itself establish improvement for that person. In the metaphorical extension of engineering, other people remain participants whose perspectives and legitimate demands can change the design.
