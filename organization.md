# The method precedes its descriptions

The central work is [a method of engineering perfect](engineering-perfect.md). Its hierarchy consists of supplied constructions and the operations, conditions, and consequences inside them. A question such as “what is worth engineering” names work still to be done; it is not a completed construction.

The method records are in [engineering-method.json](engineering-method.json). Signal restoration supplies the arithmetic operation for both known original signs. Calculation repair supplies and executes every possible answer branch. Tooling construction includes its own prerequisite. Continuous operation includes preserving actions and support. Coverage correction supplies the changed denominator and the exact consequence of the previously excluded requests.

The opening presents these completed consequences directly. Selecting one exposes its supplied operation and its internal derivation. Names identify the constructions where an identifier is useful; the generator no longer inserts a subject title and group title before the actual knowledge. The displayed consequence has the main reading size.

The readings of Engineerperfect as a perfect engineer, a method of engineering perfect, or the act of perfecting engineering remain project interpretations. They are separate from the main method. The method's actual use can improve an engineer's supported capability without proving independent learning or universal perfection.

The earlier organization and its acceptance failure remain in [the correction record](evidence/completion-correction.md) and [historical organization](evidence/organization-before-completion.json). The [original material audit](evidence/organization-audit.md) records the previous classification; it no longer governs the method. A successful archive or link check has no authority to certify semantic completion or naming convergence.
