# Organizing the work without mistaking the view for its subject

The four opening questions remain useful: what is worth engineering, what makes it possible, what keeps it possible, and what improves engineering. They organize access to the current work. They are not a declaration that reality has four exclusive departments.

The former website stopped at twelve Markdown files. Each file mixed claims, conditions, examples, dependencies, and sometimes descriptions of the project. Selecting a subject therefore left the hierarchy and entered a linear document. Making the boxes more attractive could not repair the missing distinctions.

The organization now continues through subject, question within the subject, result, and the conditions and consequences inside that result. The canonical records in [organization.json](organization.json) supply those levels. A result can occur under several relevant parents without acquiring competing copies of its content. Full arguments, source sections, and executable constructions remain available as the grounds and operations of the corresponding results.

## The main questions and their current subdivisions

| Main question | Subdivisions | Distinction formerly obscured |
|---|---|---|
| What is worth engineering | Goals, values and worthwhile change; beneficiaries, demands and authority; attention, interpretation and evidence | Discovering the purpose differs from executing a settled target. A true account need not retain governing authority. |
| What makes it possible | Alternatives and missing operations; parts, handoffs and shared conditions; observation, memory and action | Available possibilities, causal prerequisites, and usable distinctions are different conditions. |
| What keeps it possible | Arrival, persistence and recovery; production, support and reproduction; feedback, repair and revision | First success, continuing service, recovery, replacement and correction have different dependencies. |
| What improves engineering | Inquiry, judgment and discovery; learning, agency and enabling conditions; unoptimized mind change and perspective | A person's understanding, motivation, feeling, control, supported performance and retained skill are not interchangeable. |

These are present findings, not a template requiring every future subject to have three children or every result to end at a fixed depth. Add a distinction when it changes an answer, applicable condition, available contribution, or reconstruction effort. Stop subdividing when the actual relation and operation are explicit; empty levels do not deepen understanding.

## The relations a single exclusive tree loses

Refinement asks which further distinctions a subject contains. Prerequisite asks which condition an operation needs. Evidential support asks which observation or premise grounds a claim. Specialization asks which case satisfies the premises of a more general result. These are different relations. A dependency can be shared by several branches, and feedback can form a cycle.

The same canonical records therefore support three additional views: what changes in a mind; what depends on what; and what establishes which claim. Attention, interpretation, values, goals, feeling, motivation, memory, understanding, learning, agency and trust enter the substantive work where their distinctions change the contribution. They are not placed in a disconnected philosophy appendix.

The finite planners and controllers remain completed operations within their premises. They cannot define the outer scope by admitting only undertakings whose goals and states have already been fixed. [Mind change and its conditions](mind-change-and-its-conditions.md) develops the actual missing consequences, including a complete remembered-mode construction.

## Project interpretations are a different kind of content

The name's readings, project purpose statements, repository comparison, contributor rules, and design rationale describe or govern this project. They are not substantive children of “what is worth engineering” merely because they contain words about purpose. The website puts them in About Engineerperfect.

Mixed files are classified by their contents. The opening readings of “engineering and perfection” belong with interpretations. Its later deductions about the scope of a perfection claim, the inadequacy of an operation inventory, and hidden support consumption remain substantive. The original source document is retained intact; the canonical hierarchy selects its useful distinctions without silently rewriting provenance.

Likewise, evidence is attached to claims, worked cases instantiate results, and code supplies operations. None is discarded merely because its file name fails to fit a subject tree. [The material audit](evidence/organization-audit.md) records the placement of the existing sources and the differences that could otherwise be lost.

## The correction changes the producer

The active website generator reads this repository's canonical organization records. It generates the top-level view, every subject, the internal result hierarchies, and the alternative views from those records. The project description is no longer the root of the work. Contextual return controls preserve the chosen parent without cloning the result.

This applies mindchangeresearch's answer-preservation and reconstruction criteria to the actual organization. Relevant Subject Systems supplied selection of the missing distinctions, comparison with a deeper file tree and a fixed technical taxonomy, construction of the alternative view, and correction of the active producer. No original RSI or GOSM execution is implied. No static organization establishes measured beneficial mind change or universal optimality.
