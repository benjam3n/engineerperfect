# Unoptimized mind-change methods

Engineerperfect is organized around fast, beneficial, efficient changes in what minds can notice, understand, question, value, and do. Engineering includes supplying the conditions that make these changes useful, possible, and sustainable. A fast agreement with a bad proposal fails this purpose. A correct explanation that leaves the crucial operation unavailable can also fail it.

These analyses identify particular substitutions for that purpose and implement their corrections in the working content. They do not constitute a separate systems-engineering program. The examined methods include this repository's own earlier choices.

## Agreement can increase while the undertaking gets worse

William Donaldson's INCOSE-hosted [Digital Transformation using MBSE: Notes from the field](https://www.incose.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/webinar_140_donaldson_digital_transformation_using_mbse_notes_from_the_field.pdf), presented on 15 July 2020, explicitly discusses changing beliefs, assumptions, and mental models. PDF pages 50–55 discuss beneficial change, resistance, adoption, and a sequence attributed to Beer, Eisenstat, and Spector. That sequence includes “Replace non-compliers” and subsequently monitoring compliance. The presentation also discusses joint diagnosis and new competencies. It is one invited presentation, not a statement of everything INCOSE prescribes.

The consequential weakness appears when adoption or compliance becomes the test of the proposed change. In the [service example](examples/service-during-repair.md), one worker cannot sustain one service every period because repair needs the same labor. Suppose the proposed transformation promises to solve this through a new dispatch procedure without changing that arrangement. An engineer objects using the resource bound. Removing the objector can increase the fraction of remaining participants who agree. It cannot change the bound or enable the fifth successive service from the fully fresh two-station start.

This is a complete counterexample to agreement being sufficient evidence of improvement. It also identifies the beneficial direction of mind change: the proposal's advocates need to recognize the missing concurrent repair contribution. The dissenter does not need to become more accepting of an impossible schedule.

An accepted plan can of course require coordinated execution. The additional obligation is to preserve a route through which a consequential objection changes the plan. In this repository, that route reaches the equations, selected mechanisms, recommendations, and entry points that use the defeated premise. [Engineering capability](engineering-capability.md) implements that distinction; acknowledging a correction is not its application.

## A completed framework can leave the reader to perform the decisive work

A document can correctly name context, alternatives, synthesis, validation, and learning while leaving its user to discover which condition matters, construct the alternative, and decide what the evidence changes. The source-specific examples and their qualifications are in [unoptimized perspectives](unoptimized-perspectives.md). Naming a relevant operation can orient an experienced reader, but it is not equivalent to supplying that operation to somebody who lacks it.

Compare two contributions to the water undertaking. One instructs the reader to choose enough observations and a suitable control strategy. The other supplies the decision: observe whether level is at most 4, add 2 when it is, and add 0 otherwise. In the stipulated dynamics, every resulting level remains between 1 and 9. The [water example](examples/water-level-control.md) gives the proof, the narrower-target contrast, and the executable construction.

The second contribution removes a specific mathematical selection task from the recipient's remaining work. It does not establish that a person read, understood, trusted, remembered, or used it. Those effects require evidence of that person's response. The capability supplied and the human change observed are different claims.

For philosophical work, a comparable defect is telling the reader to distinguish a claim from its assumptions while leaving the consequential assumption unidentified. In the service recommendation above, the missing premise is spare concurrent labor. Identifying it and deriving its absence changes which proposal remains defensible. The contribution finishes an inference rather than prescribing an attitude toward reasoning.

## More information and immediate progress can both select the wrong work

The instruction to determine the missing contribution still left an operation unsupplied in this repository: choosing whether to inspect further, which inspection to perform, or when to intervene using what is already established. [Choosing the next contribution](choosing-the-next-contribution.md) now supplies the exact selection and its conditional branches for explicit finite cases.

Its calculation repair completes the undertaking for 2 work units. Beginning with an inquiry that reveals more bits costs 3 and improves none of the repaired outputs. Its sign case also defeats the opposite shortcut: requiring an immediate improvement from each observation rejects both first steps of a three-unit route in favor of a four-unit reset. The complete continuation establishes the observations' contribution.

These are specific failures of selection criteria, not evidence that information or visible progress is generally bad. The replacements affect the [information analysis](information-and-control.md), [capability analysis](engineering-capability.md), working instructions, and executable planner. The same sign case then constructs an omitted operation that makes the earlier diagnostic optimum unnecessary. Our own optimized plan remains answerable to a better account of the available possibilities.

## Changing a description can leave the obstruction intact

Recognizing a missing permission does not grant it. Understanding a repair requirement does not provide a repairer. An elegant map of dependencies can be accurate while every relevant action remains unavailable.

Let A be the available actions and G the worthwhile outcomes. If no action sequence admitted by A reaches G, improving a description of A alone cannot make such a sequence exist. Description may enable discovery of a previously overlooked action; that is a different case, because the actor's usable alternatives change. Where the causal route is genuinely absent, a resource, mechanism, permission, or legitimate revision of the undertaking must supply it.

The [capability analysis](engineering-capability.md) therefore distinguishes noticing an alternative, being able to execute it, and retaining the support it needs. The [tooling case](examples/tooling-and-production.md) supplies the omitted bootstrap operation and its cost. The [maintenance analysis](production-and-maintenance.md) counts recurring expert correction as a consumed contribution rather than crediting the final answer with independent reproducibility.

This broadens mind-change work beyond replacing propositions. Attention, interpretation, interest, trust, practiced capability, available action, and the conditions of continued use can matter. Which contribution is needed must be determined in the actual undertaking; assigning every failure to belief is another restrictive perspective.

## Fast change requires the useful consequence to arrive in time

Speed concerns when a justified contribution becomes usable, not merely how soon an agreeable sentence appears. Efficiency concerns the recipient's unnecessary work and the undertaking's resources, including the effort displaced onto other people and later correction.

Suppose a decision is due at time D. A contribution becomes usable only after it arrives, is interpreted sufficiently for this use, and supplies its missing dependencies. If those sequential durations are t_a, t_i, and t_s, it can support this decision only when t_a + t_i + t_s ≤ D. A shorter document can fail that condition if it leaves a long reconstruction task. A longer worked example can satisfy it if the needed result is immediately accessible and the derivation supports inspection.

No durations for human reading are measured here. The equation identifies which latency matters; it does not estimate it. Where activities overlap, the relevant duration follows their dependency path rather than an indiscriminate sum.

There is also no universal rule that immediate output settles the comparison. The [tooling calculation](examples/tooling-and-production.md) supplies an exact resource example: its bootstrap operation produces no finished unit, but enables lower total cost for four or more units under the stated model. That calculation does not measure calendar speed. An inquiry can similarly require a developed alternative before its contribution is sound. The appropriate horizon belongs to the concern, including later correction and transfer when they matter.

The repository applies this result structurally. Its README starts with consequential findings and direct routes to their constructions. Individual examples expose the decisive result before their full calculation. Definitions remain available when they help. No compulsory introductory taxonomy or identical reflection form precedes every useful contribution.

## A vocabulary change does not demonstrate a better perspective

Calling an account optimized, perfected, philosophical, or comprehensive does not determine what it lets somebody notice or do. Renaming requirements as concerns while preserving the same excluded beneficiaries leaves the original defect intact. Renaming adoption as mind change while rewarding the same compliant behavior also leaves it intact.

The comparison needs the actual differing consequence. In the service case, dispatch-only optimization cannot supply a sustainable policy; restoring concurrent repair can. In the fit case, another nominal diameter remains infeasible; changing production variation creates an explicit feasible interval. In the water case, requesting complete measurement is unnecessary for the wider band; the common-action construction supplies a coarser sufficient observation.

Each replacement defeats a specific inference under stated conditions. None earns universal superiority from the word perfection. The source names are useful for locating the defective inference and preserving its context, not for determining which side must win.

## Stored corrections need to change what produces the next result

Suppose a generator uses rule r to produce recommendation y. A critique of r is stored elsewhere, but the generator still uses r. The critique's existence does not change y. The necessary connection is replacement or restriction of r in the generator and any dependent uses that would otherwise repeat its consequence.

This applies directly to the earlier organization of engineerperfect. Its technical subjects were useful, but its opening purpose did not make beneficial efficient mind change govern their selection and arrangement. The subsequent suggestion of a separate systems-engineering area would have introduced a parallel organizing center. This revision changes the actual README, purpose, contribution instructions, subject arguments, and examples that govern continued work.

| Earlier organizing choice | Implemented consequence of the correction |
|---|---|
| Lead with the territory of engineering | [README](README.md) begins with the governing mind-change purpose and routes directly to useful findings |
| Add an institution-shaped program | These two analytical subjects explain corrections used inside the existing work |
| Treat criticism as a declaration | [Requirements](requirements-and-consequences.md), [information](information-and-control.md), [alternatives](designing-missing-possibilities.md), [composition](compatible-parts-and-processes.md), [maintenance](production-and-maintenance.md), and [capability](engineering-capability.md) use the resulting distinctions |
| Assume future contributors will infer the changed priority | [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) retains its trigger, scope, and required operation: beneficial useful change governs organization; critique must alter affected contributions |

These are inspectable changes to the repository and this contribution. Their retention in files does not prove every future author will apply them or that a reader has benefited. [Verification](evidence/verification.md) distinguishes these completed changes, the logical constructions, and the effects still requiring observation.

The resulting competition is concrete: make specified inadequate methods unnecessary by supplying what their users would otherwise have to discover, construct, or repeatedly repair. Where an incumbent supplies a useful operation, use and credit it. Where our replacement leaves the same work undone, the criticism applies to us as well.
