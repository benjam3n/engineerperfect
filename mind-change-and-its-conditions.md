# Mind change and its conditions

A worthwhile change can concern what a person notices, understands, cares about, imagines, remembers, feels, wants, can do, or controls. These are not interchangeable names for belief revision. The conditions that permit those activities can also require engineering. The distinctions below supply different consequences for selecting a contribution; they do not treat every human change as a controllable state transition.

## A method is not the goal

If method M requires prerequisite p, then successful use of M requires p. It does not follow that the goal of M requires p. That stronger inference needs every adequate route to require p.

The [sign construction](examples/correcting-composed-signs.md) supplies the counterexample. With the original operations, the least costly diagnostic route inspects two sign transformations and then corrects the delivered value, at cost 3. For a positive original value and magnitude-preserving transformations, taking the delivered magnitude restores the original directly at cost 1. That supplied route defeats the claim that this repair requires diagnosis. For a negative original value it fails; the positive-source condition remains essential.

This changes how an objection should be interpreted. Rejection of a costly, unavailable, or unauthorized method need not reject its goal. The missing contribution can be an adequate alternative, not stronger motivation to use the original method. This application follows mindchangeresearch's distinction between a goal's prerequisites and a proposed method's prerequisites. [Source: goals](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/goals.md).

## Inquiry can form its purpose

An instruction requiring a settled target before inquiry excludes inquiry whose authorized purpose is to discover a worthwhile target. Executing that instruction more faithfully cannot repair the exclusion. Execution of an established change, discovery of a useful change, and reconsideration of the criterion are different undertakings.

The account that loses governing authority need not become false. A provider can accurately report completing every accepted request while that question ceases to be adequate for assessing whether eligible people receive service. The [coverage construction](requirements-and-consequences.md) supplies the different denominator and its consequence. Likewise, an optimizer can be exactly right about its original inventory while a newly constructed operation makes that inventory inadequate for the wider undertaking.

These examples preserve what the old account establishes and develop the changed question far enough to affect judgment. Renaming the account without changing a relevant question, distinction, operation, or conclusion would not supply this improvement. [Source: exploration and goal change](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/findings/exploration-and-goal-change.md), [imagination](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/imagination.md).

## Conflicting values and bundled actions

Suppose the live obligations are to preserve the substantive content of dissent for review and protect the contributor's identity when identification is unnecessary. Publishing content and identity together violates the latter obligation. Suppressing both violates the former. This two-option inventory makes the obligations appear incompatible.

Separating content, identity, contact route, authority, and expiry exposes a different operation: retain the substantive account for review while withholding unnecessary identifying fields. Under the stated obligations, that operation preserves both. It does not show that every privacy or accountability conflict disappears. If identification itself is a legitimate requirement, that different demand must remain in the comparison. Nor does the constructed case establish that a person's values changed. It establishes a possible correction to the alternatives through which those values are served.

The engineering consequence is to inspect the bundled action before treating a collision between current options as an unavoidable conflict of values. This reuses the constructed distinction in [mindchangeresearch: values](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/values.md). Its relation to physical construction is developed independently in [designing missing possibilities](designing-missing-possibilities.md).

## Efficiency depends on what is valued

Consider route A, which finishes a task in one effort unit without an additional valued distinction, and route B, which finishes it in two units and supplies that distinction. If only task completion matters, A is preferable on the supplied comparison. If the extra understanding is worth the additional effort, B can be preferable. Task completion and effort alone do not settle the ranking when understanding is also valued.

The same applies to curiosity, a changed goal, the enjoyment of inquiry, or retained capability. They need not be justified exclusively as means to an already fixed output. Conversely, calling an experience valuable does not establish that it outweighs its costs or burdens on others.

This result prevents “minimize mind change” from silently replacing “maximize beneficial mind change for the relevant effort.” For instrumental reuse, the actual later uses, maintenance, expiry, reconstruction, and opportunity costs matter. The [tooling calculation](examples/tooling-and-production.md) supplies a separate exact continuation comparison, including its bootstrap contribution. [Sources: exploration and goal change](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/findings/exploration-and-goal-change.md), [time, effort, and value](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/findings/time-effort-and-value.md).

## Attention and memory need receiving operations

A display containing accepted requests and completed requests can support an accurate completion judgment while omitting the eligible requests that were never accepted. Exposing eligible requests beside served requests supplies the distinction needed to notice the coverage shortfall. Asking the recipient to pay more attention does not supply an absent denominator.

Memory adds a different dependency. A correction can be retained in an archive without being retrieved at the relevant decision. It can be retrieved without its applicability condition. Remembering “take absolute value” while forgetting the positive-source condition can reproduce the retained negative-source failure.

The useful representation therefore keeps the operation, its trigger, and its consequential boundary recoverable together. The correction must reach the receiving operation: a planner's admitted action, a generator's selection rule, or a person's actual use. File existence establishes storage, not timely retrieval, learning, or uptake. This distinction changes both [information and control](information-and-control.md) and [correction propagation](unoptimized-mind-change-methods.md).

Sources: [attention](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/attention.md), [memory](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/memory.md). These are consequences about supplied distinctions and dependencies; they do not measure a reader's attention or recall.

## Remembered history can change the available control

Consider a stipulated device with persistent mode L or R. An initial observation reveals the mode. Every later observation is the same ready signal. Action L succeeds only in mode L; action R succeeds only in mode R. The mode never changes.

A memoryless policy using only the later ready signal must select the same action in both modes, so it fails in one. A policy retaining the initial observation selects the action named by that observation. It succeeds at every later step because the mode persists. If the mode can change unobserved, the remembered initial observation no longer establishes the current mode and the guarantee fails.

This completed finite counterexample shows why failure of a current-observation intersection criterion cannot prove impossibility for every history-dependent controller. It adds no claim that a general hidden-state controller has been implemented. The original control tools retain their stated full-state and memoryless scope. The distinction comes from [information and control](information-and-control.md#memory-can-create-additional-usable-distinctions); its selection rule is fully specified here for this two-mode example.

## Performance, learning, and transfer are different

A planner can supply a correct route for an explicit finite problem without establishing that its recipient learned to construct such routes. Supported performance concerns the person with the aid and its available dependencies. Retained skill concerns what the person can later discriminate or execute under the relevant conditions. Transfer concerns another receiving context and the premises it actually satisfies.

These are different accomplishments, not a ranking that always favors unsupported performance. If continued supported capability is the purpose, keeping the tool, access, maintenance, data, and correction route available can be the appropriate construction. If later independent use is required, successful output with the aid leaves that obligation unestablished. Removing support can remove the only demonstrated capability.

This distinction prevents [reproduction](production-and-maintenance.md) from crediting a recipient with an author's hidden contribution and prevents [engineering capability](engineering-capability.md) from equating understanding, action, and available conditions. Sources: [action](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/action.md), [understanding](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/understanding.md), [memory](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/memory.md).

## Motivation, emotion, and external conditions

“I do not want this method,” “I want the outcome but cannot act,” and “I do not want the outcome” support different inquiries. A rejected route can be infeasible, unauthorized, too costly, or inferior to another route. Nonperformance under a blocked arrangement is compatible with willingness. In the service case, encouragement cannot supply the missing concurrent repair labor.

A feeling, its interpretation, and the external condition associated with it are also distinct targets. A report of distress does not by itself establish its cause or the operation needed. If a contribution was lost, restoring the contribution and changing the contributor's interpretation are different operations. Reappraisal that leaves the loss intact cannot be credited with preserving the work. Restoring the work does not, by itself, establish an emotional improvement either.

These distinctions require the contribution to address the live concern while preserving the person's report and actual control. They defeat a blanket choice of persuasion or belief correction as the remedy for every failure to act. Sources: [motivation](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/motivation.md), [emotion](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/emotion.md).

## Disagreement does not have one repair

Two claims can differ in their subject, period, reference class, or meaning before their truth is compared. “Every request was completed” and “most eligible requests were not served” can both hold when the first concerns accepted requests. The repair is to expose the different denominators, not force one speaker to retract a true claim.

Two people can also agree on the calculation while differing over goals, permissible burdens, decision rights, or methods. More accurate arithmetic does not, by itself, choose whose concern governs or grant authority to decide. Preserve the actual difference until a justified judgment or new alternative addresses it. A framework that rewards coherent language can hide a conflict it has not resolved.

This corrects [requirements](requirements-and-consequences.md), [composition](compatible-parts-and-processes.md), and the treatment of agreement in [unoptimized mind-change methods](unoptimized-mind-change-methods.md). Source: [disagreement](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/disagreement.md).

## Trust and evidence have distinct dependencies

If two dashboards copy one event record, agreement between them survives an error in that record. Counting the dashboards as independent corroboration fails to check the shared source. Likewise, two arguments that both require premise p do not provide a route independent of p. Defeating p defeats those routes without proving the conclusion false.

Sincerity, access to the event, competence, reliability of the route, and permission to disclose answer different questions. A sincere person can lack direct access; a reliable report can still be unauthorized to publish. A single person-wide trust score loses those distinctions.

The engineering consequence is to inspect the actual evidential dependency and the authority of the actual operation. This reuses the dependency distinction in [mindchangeresearch: trust](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/trust.md) alongside the Boolean construction in [compatible parts and processes](compatible-parts-and-processes.md).

## Feedback has a deadline

Suppose a corrective action must begin by 09:20 to meet its live obligation, and an accurate report arrives at 09:25. Even immediate comprehension leaves the report too late to support that action. Better wording alone cannot restore the expired possibility.

Earlier detection, an earlier usable report, a valid faster action, continuing reserve capacity, or an authorized revised deadline can change the available continuation. Each requires its actual construction. The original deadline remains missed even if a later undertaking succeeds.

This case separates factual correctness from useful timing. It also changes the relevant organization: feedback is a dependency of action and continued service, not just a file after a completed project. Source: [mindchangeresearch: feedback](https://github.com/benjam3n/mindchangeresearch/blob/eeab4b4b54415d436fc838a4e8546403269dd9b3/changes/feedback.md). [Timing and support](compatible-parts-and-processes.md) and [usable consequences](unoptimized-mind-change-methods.md) supply the receiving engineering relations.

## One hierarchy cannot preserve every relation

For a representation r and a family of questions Q, exact answer preservation requires that whenever r(x) = r(y), every question in Q has the same answer on x and y. Otherwise a decoder using only r receives the same input where different answers are required. Keeping reachability can still lose shortest distance; keeping an answer somewhere can still leave costly reconstruction before it is usable.

Hierarchical refinement, causal prerequisite, shared support, instance of a general result, evidential support, and changes through time are different relations. Shared dependencies can have several parents; feedback can form cycles. A tree can organize access to these relations, but the additional nesting must not silently turn all of them into containment or force an item into one exclusive location.
