# Contributing to engineerperfect

The current user request governs. Organize engineerperfect around fast, beneficial, efficient changes in what minds can notice, understand, question, value, and do. Supply the conditions that make those changes useful, possible, and sustainable. Preserve what the recipient has already established and supply the missing contribution with the least unnecessary delay and recipient effort. Agreement, compliance, a document, and a declared optimization do not by themselves establish beneficial mind change.

The scope includes the literal practice of engineering, the broader act of arranging worthwhile conditions, improvement of engineering itself, and the engineer's actual capabilities. A live inquiry can form or change its subject, interpretation, beneficiary, target, mechanism, available possibilities, or criterion. Do not require these to be settled before inquiry begins or treat a professional boundary as the limit of relevant causes. Use a formal model when its terms support the determination; do not impose one on every subject.

Read the relevant existing result before extending it. The repository comparison and pinned source record distinguish prior work from additions here. Do not present inherited distinctions, familiar engineering methods, or imported results as new discoveries.

Supply the requested construction, comparison, derivation, intervention, or exact conditional result. If a central operation is named but not supplied, implement or perform it when possible. If a missing parameter permits a symbolic answer, derive that answer. An unavailable observation may limit a claim; it does not excuse omitting consequences already entailed by the available facts.

When choosing between further inquiry and an intervention is the missing contribution, use the completed construction in [choosing the next contribution](choosing-the-next-contribution.md) where its assumptions fit. Preserve already established context, compare complete relevant continuations, and distinguish a common adequate action from the least costly justified route. Do not treat information count as a sufficient criterion for contribution or require each intermediate contribution to improve the final recommendation immediately. Do not invent case coverage or costs to force a numerical answer. The case inventory and available operations remain revisable; a new answer or a constructed alternative must change the affected plan, as performed in the examples.

When a live weakness concerns consequence construction, capability construction, alternatives, or correction uptake, use the relevant Subject System already identified in the pinned mindchangeresearch instructions. Perform its missing contribution or reuse a justified applicable result. Naming the system does not satisfy this requirement. Original RSI/Reasoningtool procedures require their own original sources if explicitly invoked; no original-skill execution is implied here.

Use actual subject names and direct findings. A heading, schema, repeated reflection paragraph, priority number, or placeholder phrase cannot stand in for the missing judgment. Organize by the useful changes and dependencies the material actually supports, without mandatory opening fields, uniform reflection sections, or an inherited disciplinary sequence. Put the consequential result where it can be used and give the reasoning needed to assess it. Keep sources and verification accounting out of the main argument except where they establish an essential claim.

The current organization is represented in `organization.json`. Preserve hierarchy below the top-level subjects: a source document is not automatically an indivisible leaf. Separate project interpretations and contributor instructions from substantive work; split mixed documents by their actual contents when representing them, while preserving full source provenance. Keep one canonical result when several parents or perspectives need it. Distinguish refinement, causal prerequisite, shared support, case, evidence, and feedback rather than treating every link as containment. The number of current roots, subjects, or result parts is not a mandatory taxonomy. Add a level only when it supplies a necessary distinction or operation.

Use `mind-change-and-its-conditions.md` where applicable. Do not organize all work as action selection after goals are fixed. Purpose, value, attention, interpretation, memory, emotion, motivation, imagination, agency, and the enabling conditions can be live subjects of change. Preserve the differences between an implemented operation, supported performance, retained learning, and observed human benefit. A true initial account may lose governing authority without being refuted.

Examine established frameworks through unoptimized perspectives and unoptimized mind-change methods. Their only role here is to inform changes in the working project. Do not add a separate systems-engineering program or let an institution's categories govern the repository. Identify an actual source passage, its relevant qualification, the failing inference or missing operation, and the supplied correction in the affected content. A critique cannot remain an appendix while its defect persists in the active instructions or products. Apply this requirement to engineerperfect too. Source mentions do not establish adequacy; silence in selected passages does not prove field-wide absence. Claims of superiority need a specified comparison and supported consequence.

Separate a worthwhile requirement, a feasible mechanism, faithful execution, observed effect, continued maintenance, and transfer. A passing test establishes the property tested. An empty action or successor set cannot become a successful control action through vacuous logic. No automatically assigned KEEP or completion count replaces inspection of the supplied product and its obligation.

Count support resources, production prerequisites, time until a contribution becomes usable, repair, and recipient effort where they affect the undertaking. Faster agreement is not the objective. Include the relevant continuation, correction, and transfer when comparing changes. Identify a model boundary without making that boundary a universal limit on engineering. A change of criterion or admitted conditions changes the claim; preserve the original obligation when it remains live. Do not force incompatible interests, hypotheses, or demands into a falsely coherent account.

Correct actual producers and dependent uses when a demonstrated defect is found. Retain meaningful failed constructions and counterexamples with the premises that produced them. Do not continue generating or testing after the requested work is complete unless another concrete unresolved risk requires it.

For this implementation, run the example calculation and the finite-control checks when relevant code or assumptions change. The examples are stipulated models, not empirical interventions. Do not claim field performance, changes in a person's mind, or improved general model capability without the appropriate observations.
