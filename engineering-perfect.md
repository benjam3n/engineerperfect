# A method of engineering perfect

A method that still requires its recipient to invent its decisive operation has not supplied that operation. A method whose every stated step can be followed while its promised result fails has not established that result. Complete engineering therefore includes the actual transformation, the conditions under which it succeeds, and every dependency consumed in obtaining the promised result. An engineer supplied with that complete construction does not also need to rediscover it.

## Complete operation

Let S contain the situations still possible, K(s) the available operations that produce the required result in situation s, and e(s) the information received. A method using only e(s) can produce the required result in every admitted situation exactly when each indistinguishable group has a common successful operation:

\[
\forall z\in e(S),\qquad \bigcap_{s:e(s)=z}K(s)\ne\varnothing.
\]

Every situation in one group presents the same input to the method. A successful method must therefore return an operation that succeeds throughout that group. Conversely, a supplied operation in each intersection constructs such a method. The finite intersection and conditional-plan implementation already performs this selection; a recipient is not asked to identify an appropriate mechanism.

For a positive original signal x and a received value y in {x, −x}, the completed operation is y ↦ |y|. Since |x| = |−x| = x, the original is restored without either sign flag being identified. At the stated costs, this costs 1 rather than the original diagnostic optimum of 3. For original x < 0, the corresponding operation is y ↦ −|y|, which restores either received sign. With the existing one-unit absolute-value and one-unit sign-reversal operations, this composition costs 2, still below diagnosis at 3. No fused one-unit cost is assumed. When the original sign is genuinely unavailable and both originals remain possible, received magnitude alone cannot recover it: +7 and −7 can produce the same received +7 while requiring different restored values.

This completes a construction omitted from the previous website. That site treated the failure of positive magnitude for a negative original as the end of the analysis and returned to diagnosis, even when the negative original was known. Signed magnitude supplies the missing operation. The difference is an actual added capability, not a revised title. [Executed construction](tools/engineer.py).

## Complete conditional plan

For a finite possible-case set B, additive nonnegative costs, truthful inquiries with fixed answers, and repairs with supplied success sets, the least worst-case total work is the smaller of: the cost of a repair adequate throughout B; or an inquiry's cost plus the greatest minimum continuation cost among its possible answers. Every retained inquiry splits B into smaller nonempty sets. The recursion therefore terminates, constructs every answer branch, and returns an actual repair at each successful leaf. If an indistinguishable group has no common successful repair, the construction returns that exact obstruction.

The [implementation](tools/choose_contribution.py) evaluates all supplied first repairs and all distinguishing first inquiries. Induction on the number of remaining cases establishes adequacy and minimum worst-case cost. No unperformed choice is hidden in the returned plan. A common adequate repair is compared with potentially cheaper inquiry; its existence does not automatically make immediate action optimal.

For the calculation 10.06 − 10.00, one missing component can be either the rule or the input, with four layouts for each omission. The completed plan observes the missing component and installs precisely it. All eight executed repairs return 3/50. Inspection costs 1 and the repair costs 1, so total work is 2 in every case. Inspecting layout first costs 3 and restores the same value. When the omission is already known, the supplied component costs 1 and the redundant inquiry disappears. These branches are computed and executed, rather than displayed as instructions awaiting implementation.

## Construction of the means

If all available routes preserve an obstruction, selecting among those routes cannot remove it. An alternative becomes useful through a supplied different operation or changed causal condition; naming it does not add it to the available set.

For required clearance 0.02–0.10 and independent shaft and hole half-widths 0.040 and 0.050, nominal gap g must satisfy g ≥ 0.110 and g ≤ 0.010. The admissible interval is empty. With half-widths 0.010 and 0.015, it becomes 0.045 ≤ g ≤ 0.075. The supplied g = 0.060 yields actual clearance 0.035–0.085. This specifies the required manufacturing capability and nominal design; it does not claim that a physical manufacturing process was installed by the deduction.

The tooling construction closes another dependency. Manual production costs 4n. A ready tool costs 5 + n + 2 floor((n−1)/3), but the tool's initial required part must first be produced at cost 4. The complete tooling route therefore costs 9 + n + 2 floor((n−1)/3). Manual production wins for n = 1 and 2, the routes tie at n = 3, and tooling wins for every integer n ≥ 4. The missing means and its changed consequence are both supplied; “account for prerequisites” would leave these determinations undone.

## Continuing operation

A result that must continue includes its preserving operation and the continued availability of that operation. For a finite transition model, an available action has a nonempty set of possible successors. Starting with the allowed states, repeatedly removing states without an action that preserves the remaining set constructs the greatest maintainable subset. At its fixed point, the supplied preserving actions keep every admitted trajectory inside it. Expanding robust predecessors from a maintainable target constructs the states from which that target can be reached and the exact worst-case arrival layers. [Implementation](tools/finite_control.py).

For water level x in 1–9 and withdrawal d in {0,1,2}, the operation u(x) = 2 for x ≤ 4 and u(x) = 0 otherwise yields x′ = x + u(x) − d in 1–9. The complete policy needs only the low/high distinction. For arrival in 4–6, u(x) = min(3, 6−x) reaches the target within three periods and preserves it. The negative actions at high levels are admitted removal actions of this model.

For two stations that each serve twice before repair, one worker fully occupied by one required service per period cannot perform a concurrent repair. Two initially ready stations supply only four services. With a second worker, service at a usable station and simultaneous repair of the other preserves the existence of a usable station. This covers every initial wear pair except the pair where both stations are already fully worn. The completed policy includes its labor dependency and starting condition.

## Perfection of the engineer

A person's possession of the completed construction differs from their unaided ability to invent it. Supported execution, retained skill, understanding, feeling, willingness, and actual control are distinct accomplishments. For an undertaking whose concern is a correct result, repeating the construction work inside the recipient is unnecessary when the supplied operation already delivers it. Where learning or independent later performance is itself wanted, that different result remains part of the undertaking.

The method also applies to the producer of this repository. The rejected generator demanded titles, summaries, group names, and subordinate titles before displaying the actual result. Its checker accepted those fields when nonempty. Replacing every result paragraph with “determine the useful consequence” preserved those structural properties. The instructions prohibited the failure while the production route and acceptance predicate still admitted it.

The replacement gives the main hierarchy supplied constructions. Within a construction, the visible material is the derived consequence and its necessary conditions. Topic announcements no longer occupy compulsory levels before it. The calculation's selected plan is executed automatically whenever its conditions change, and signed-magnitude restoration covers both known original signs with the corresponding one- and two-unit costs. These are changes to the active producer and available operations. They do not establish a universally perfect engineer or measured human mind change.

## Revision of what the result is

The adequacy relation in these exact constructions does not choose whose concern deserves to govern. A statement that 10 of 10 accepted requests were served is compatible with 90 of 100 eligible requests remaining unserved. For the concern that eligible requests receive service, completion of accepted requests is an insufficient success criterion; eligibility rather than acceptance supplies the denominator. The corrected judgment is 10% coverage. Changing the score exposes the missing service but does not itself provide it.

Likewise, publishing the substance of dissent need not require publishing its author's unnecessary identifying details. Separating content from identity preserves the stipulated review and privacy obligations together. If identification itself is required, that different obligation changes the result. A goal, value, interpretation, or way of understanding the undertaking can therefore change without the original statements becoming false. The finite constructions above supply exact conditional methods, not authority to fix every future purpose in advance.
