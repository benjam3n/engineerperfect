# Unoptimized perspectives

A perspective can contain true propositions and still direct attention toward the wrong work. Its defect may lie in what it treats as fixed, which alternatives it permits, whose concern it represents, or which missing judgment it leaves to the reader. Correcting it can change the subject of inquiry without falsifying everything the earlier account said.

The comparisons here concern specific passages and inferences. They supply changes used in the rest of engineerperfect. INCOSE, SEBoK, and this repository can each contain useful results alongside inadequate ways of approaching a matter. An institution's name establishes neither adequacy nor failure.

| Examined perspective | Consequential defect | Replacement already used here |
|---|---|---|
| Bound an undertaking by the profession's division of work | A necessary contribution becomes somebody else's problem while its absence still defeats the outcome | Include the actual production, access, and repair dependencies in [production and maintenance](production-and-maintenance.md) |
| Treat a selected description as the necessary form of the subject | Questions, beneficiaries, and possible interventions become fixed before their adequacy is examined | Permit their formation and revision in [engineering and perfection](engineering-and-perfection.md) |
| Understand everything before making a justified intervention | Irrelevant distinctions become prerequisites | Construct sufficient distinctions in [information and control](information-and-control.md) |
| Select essential characteristics or the right combination | The instruction assumes the discrimination or construction it is supposed to supply | Supply the common-action calculation and the [actual alternative constructions](designing-missing-possibilities.md) |
| Balance named opposites | A supposed trade-off can conceal a design that improves both | Compare attainable consequences before choosing a compromise in [designing missing possibilities](designing-missing-possibilities.md) |
| Make views coherent before preserving their conflict | A disagreement that exposes an impossible undertaking can disappear into wording | Retain incompatible obligations and resolve their actual connection in [compatible parts and processes](compatible-parts-and-processes.md) |

## A professional division cannot bound the causes of success

SEBoK's [Systems Engineering Overview](https://sebokwiki.org/w/index.php?title=Systems_Engineering_Overview&oldid=77971), under its discussion of professional scope, places construction, manufacturing, funding, and general management outside systems engineering. It includes analysis of production alternatives and explicitly acknowledges that poor execution outside that scope can defeat realization. This is a division of professional work, not a claim that those activities are causally irrelevant.

The defect appears when that division governs an undertaking whose obligation is to make the result happen. Let success require analysis A and manufacture M. A professional may discharge an assignment by providing A. The undertaking still requires A ∧ M. Redefining the profession does not derive M from A or make M available.

In the [tooling case](examples/tooling-and-production.md), a production tool needs a calibration part. Excluding fabrication from the inquiry hides the cost of making that part manually. Restoring it changes the correct investment threshold: tooling first wins at four units rather than two. The boundary affects the recommendation through a concrete omitted cost.

In a philosophical inquiry the same error occurs when a justification depends on an observation that another discipline is expected to provide. An accurate account of who would supply the observation is not the observation. A conditional conclusion can remain valid, but its premise stays conditional until supported. This extension depends on a conjunction of required contributions; it does not require calling philosophy a manufacturing system.

## A chosen representation does not prescribe what exists

SEBoK's [What is Systems Thinking?](https://sebokwiki.org/w/index.php?title=What_is_Systems_Thinking%3F&oldid=78451) already describes boundaries as influenced by observers and purposes. It discusses revising boundaries, other people's perspectives, power, ethics, and the difference between models and reality. A criticism that it universally mistakes its models for reality would contradict those passages.

The remaining question is operational: which distinction, assumption, question, or intervention changes because this particular representation is inadequate? Acknowledging that models are selective leaves that determination open.

Suppose a service is described as completing every accepted request. Under that description, finishing one accepted request is complete success. If nine eligible requests were rejected, a different question—whether eligible demand was served—gives one success out of ten. The first fraction can be true. Its authority to represent the concern fails. [Requirements and consequences](requirements-and-consequences.md) repairs both the denominator and the distinction between discovering the gap and supplying missing service.

This is a philosophical change in what counts as a relevant description and a satisfactory reason. It cannot be obtained by optimizing the original fraction more precisely. Nor must an inquiry begin with an already settled system, target, or beneficiary: discovering that the original terms hide the concern may be its central contribution.

## Understanding the whole is not a prerequisite to every justified answer

The same SEBoK article presents a tension between understanding all possible relationships and future situations and doing useful work. It then permits selective attention and limited risk, with adequacy partly left to judgment. That qualification matters: the article does not insist that practitioners model everything before acting.

An unrestricted understanding and a justified answer to a particular question have different conditions. Let q(x) be an answer about circumstance x and h(x) a retained representation. An exact answer can be recovered from h precisely when q is constant on every set of circumstances sharing the same h. Necessity follows because one retained value cannot decode to two different answers. Sufficiency follows by assigning that common answer to each retained value. This is an application of the existing representation-preservation work in [Autophilosophy2](https://github.com/benjam3n/autophilosophy2/blob/a646e2edaed739e7cdcbfa3d783e7a297fbbfd7b/deductions/23-representation-preservation-under-action.md), not a new claim of priority.

For an action, exact recovery of every answer is stronger than necessary. It suffices that all still-possible circumstances admit a common adequate action. The water controller preserves levels 1–9 using only low/high information. It need not know which of nine exact levels exists at every decision. The proof and executable selection are in [information and control](information-and-control.md).

The conclusion does not license ignoring any inconvenient fact. An omitted difference that changes the required answer or removes the common adequate action invalidates this simplification. This locates the adequacy question in the intended use instead of making complete knowledge the prerequisite.

## Essential characteristics and the right combination still need to be found

SEBoK's [Principles of Systems Thinking](https://sebokwiki.org/w/index.php?title=Principles_of_Systems_Thinking&oldid=78452) recommends attention to essential characteristics, synthesis of appropriately interacting parts, integrated consistent views, and balance between some dualities. It also says that not every principle applies to every decision and assigns a role to judgment and experience. These are qualified guides. They do not themselves perform every selection they name.

A statement is a placeholder relative to an obligation when its key term assumes the contribution that obligation requires. A reminder to an expert can be useful without teaching the skill. A body of knowledge can legitimately direct further reading. But if the current undertaking is to supply an adequate controller, the instruction to choose the right action has not supplied one.

Here that operation is completed: construct the safe action set at each admitted state, intersect those sets within each observation class, reject empty intersections, and select a member of every remaining intersection. The finite implementation performs these operations; the water example supplies actual actions. The determining information, the selection, and its use are available together.

The same test applies to our own phrases. Perspective optimization, beneficial change, and perfection identify work. Their names cannot establish that a worthwhile perspective has been constructed or that a recipient has improved.

## A named duality does not establish a trade-off

The principles page describes power and generality as a trade-off and discusses balancing dual characteristics. Its qualification about applicability prevents treating every such statement as a universal law. Even so, neither the names of two desirable properties nor examples of conflict derive an unavoidable conflict in a new undertaking.

Consider two available designs with pairs of desirable outcomes (1,1) and (0,0), where larger is better in each coordinate and other relevant conditions are equal. The first improves both. There is no reason to sacrifice either property to balance the pair. With only (1,0) and (0,1) available, a conflict exists within that pair of alternatives. Adding an attainable (1,1) removes it. The difference is in the feasible designs, not the vocabulary.

In the fit example, changing nominal dimensions cannot resolve the conflict between variation and clearance. Changing manufacturing variation creates designs that satisfy both clearance limits. [Designing missing possibilities](designing-missing-possibilities.md) now uses the existence of the conflict to identify a changeable premise before asking for compromise.

This does not make every conflict removable. It establishes that an actual restriction or incompatible demand must support a claim of necessity. Calling something a duality supplies neither.

## Coherence can conceal the disagreement that identifies the defect

The principles page's requirement for integrated consistent views is appropriate for descriptions jointly asserted of the same matter under the same conditions. Extending it to every perspective under consideration prematurely discards competing hypotheses, incompatible demands, and different beneficiaries' judgments.

Suppose one account requires a worker to serve a customer throughout an interval, while another requires that worker to repair equipment throughout the same interval. Each account is understandable. Combining their terminology into a coherent process description cannot supply concurrent labor. Preserving the conflict reveals the missing capacity; the [service calculation](examples/service-during-repair.md) determines the consequence.

Similarly, two people may assign different values to the same outcome without either misdescribing its physical properties. Combining their views into one score requires a justified rule for resolving those values. Until supplied, disagreement is information about the undertaking. It is not an inconsistency that can be edited away.

## Transfer follows the premises that make the operation work

SEBoK's [Guidance for Engineers](https://sebokwiki.org/w/index.php?title=Guidance_for_Engineers&oldid=78239) organizes learning around professional topics and illustrates use through an engineer's enterprise and product concerns. That is a legitimate audience. It does not define the full applicability of the relations used in those topics. The reviewed systems-thinking material itself includes social and philosophical concerns.

The broader applications here follow explicit conditions. A retained answer that erases an action-changing distinction can fail in a philosophical argument as well as a controller. A personal undertaking that needs two simultaneous uses of exclusive time has the same resource conflict as the service example when those timing premises hold. Two purportedly independent reasons that both depend on one unsupported premise retain that dependency, just as two physical routes can share an unavailable supply.

These are completed logical transfers, not evidence that people follow a finite-state machine or that a technique succeeds everywhere. A new application can require forming a different question, representing a different dependency, or observing a different effect. Its disciplinary name supplies no exemption and no automatic guarantee.

The repository's [unoptimized mind-change methods](unoptimized-mind-change-methods.md) examines what these corrections require of an actual contribution, including our own writing, organization, and continued use.
